DRUM BREAKER by HIDDEN AUDIO

Windows 10/11, 64-bit VST3 instrument

Close your DAW and the standalone app before installation or updating.
The VST3 instrument installs to the system's Common Files\VST3 folder.
Scan for new plugins in your DAW, then load Drum Breaker as an instrument.
The standalone app and desktop shortcut are optional installer selections.

ACTIVATION
A valid DrumBreaker Lemon Squeezy key and internet access are required for
activation and startup verification. Playback and WAV export remain locked
until verification succeeds. There is no offline grace period.

Activation is stored for the current Windows user in Credential Manager.
Updates and uninstall do not remove that record or use a new activation slot.
Uninstall does not deactivate a license in Lemon Squeezy.

PROJECTS AND EXPORTS
Keep original sample files at their saved locations; project state currently
references them rather than embedding the audio. Existing samples, DAW projects,
user settings, and exported WAV files are not removed by this installer.

EXPORT TO PLAYLIST renders a WAV drag handle. Drag that file into your DAW's
timeline; the plugin does not automatically create a host track or insert a clip.
